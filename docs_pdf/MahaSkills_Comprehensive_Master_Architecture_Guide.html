Chapter 1: The Maharashtra Vocational Crisis & Problem Statement #26134

Product Domain
💡 Plain English Concept — What is this project and why is it desperately needed?
Imagine going to a government vocational school for two years to learn how to fix petrol engines, only to graduate and discover that every automotive factory in your district (like Tata Motors or Bajaj in Pune) has switched entirely to electric vehicles and robotic assembly lines! This is the exact crisis facing youth in Maharashtra today.

The state operates over 417 Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) training 1.5 lakh students every year. But their syllabi are revised manually only once every 5 years by government committees. Meanwhile, modern industry introduces new software, tools, and machines every few months. Because of this massive “curriculum drift,” more than half of all vocational graduates cannot find jobs, while employers complain they cannot find skilled workers.

MahaSkills is an autonomous digital platform that acts as a real-time bridge. It automatically reads live job listings across the state, calculates exactly which skills are needed in each district, and generates the exact administrative paperwork for government officials to update courses in 21 days instead of 5 years.
⚙️ Terminology & Key Governance Entities
  • ITI (Industrial Training Institute): State-run post-secondary vocational training institutes delivering certificate trades (Fitter, Electrician, Welder, Machinist, COPA).
  • DSEEI & DVET: Department of Skills, Employment, Entrepreneurship and Innovation; and Directorate of Vocational Education and Training (Government of Maharashtra).
  • MSInS: Maharashtra State Innovation Society, driving technological entrepreneurship and hackathon deployment.
  • NSQF (National Skills Qualification Framework): A competency-based framework organizing qualifications from levels 1 to 10 based on knowledge, skills, and aptitude.
  • SSC (Sector Skill Council): Industry-led autonomous bodies (e.g., ASDC for Automotive, NASSCOM for IT) that set occupational standards.
  • LMI (Labour Market Intelligence): Real-time quantitative aggregation of job vacancies, hiring velocities, wage premiums, and skill demand signals.
🏛️ The Administrative Mandate (Problem Statement #26134)
Issued directly by DSEEI / MSInS, Problem Statement 26134 mandates the creation of a closed-loop platform that detects emerging skill demand at the district level, identifies obsolete training courses, and auto-generates evidence-grounded curriculum dossiers and annual district training plans.

Chapter 2: The Core Innovation — Closed-Loop Alignment Architecture

End-to-End Lifecycle
💡 Plain English Concept — What does “Closing the Loop” mean?
Traditional government portals like National Career Service (NCS) or job boards operate as an “open loop”: they show job listings, candidates apply, but the vocational schools never learn anything from those job ads! The schools keep teaching the same outdated 2018 syllabus forever.

MahaSkills “closes the loop”: when hiring for “Battery Management Systems” or “Solar PV Inverters” spikes in Pune or Nagpur, our system automatically traces backward into the relevant ITI trade, flags that the current syllabus lacks these modules, drafts the revised lesson plans, routes them to officials for digital sign-off, updates the classroom curriculum, and tracks whether the next batch of students gets hired at higher salaries!
Diagram 1: End-to-End Closed-Loop Alignment Architecture Source: docs/01-product/PRD.md & docs/02-architecture/SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md
The complete closed-loop lifecycle: market signals are parsed, scored, synthesized into curriculum modifications, ratified by state review bodies, executed across 417+ ITIs, and continually evaluated via verified placement returns.
⚙️ Step-by-Step Technical Execution of the 6 Stages
  1. Step 1 (Ingest): Nightly Apache Airflow scrapers ingest public postings from NCS, Naukri, and LinkedIn across 36 district geo-bounds, alongside monthly ITI placement CSV uploads.
  2. Step 2 (Map): Natural Language Processing tokenizes unstructured job text and maps messy titles (“CNC Specialist”) to canonical NSQF job roles and NOS (National Occupational Standard) units.
  3. Step 3 (Score): Proprietary mathematical Gap Engine calculates a 0.00–1.00 score combining vacancy velocity, wage premiums, curriculum semantic drift, and historical placement rates.
  4. Step 4 (Dossier): Automatically synthesizes an NCVET-compliant Curriculum Modification Dossier complete with 12-month vacancy trends, hiring employer rosters, and syllabus diffs.
  5. Step 5 (Ratify): Routes through an administrative state machine: Sector Skill Council (SSC) technical validation → DSEEI Joint Secretary digital approval.
  6. Step 6 (Act & Evaluate): Sanctions updated ITI seat quotas, allocates workshop capex budgets, schedules faculty retraining, and measures outcome uplift via subsequent placement returns.
🏛️ Continuous Evaluation & Governance Feedback Loop
The feedback loop does not terminate at syllabus publication. Every month, verified ITI placement returns feed back into the scoring engine to evaluate whether the curriculum revision produced actual wage uplift and higher hiring velocity. If placement falls short, scoring weights recalibrate automatically.

MahaSkills is designed like a modern, enterprise banking application. Instead of one giant, fragile program where a single bug crashes everything, the platform is divided into three completely decoupled tiers: a lightweight, lightning-fast frontend that works on mobile phones in rural talukas; a high-performance backend API that processes data asynchronously; and an intelligent database layer that combines regular data tables with AI vector search.

Chapter 4: The Mathematical Core — Gap Scoring & Obsolescence Engine

Algorithmic IP
💡 Plain English Concept — How do we mathematically detect an obsolete course without human bias?
If a human committee decides which vocational courses to shut down or fund, personal politics and lobbying can influence decisions. MahaSkills removes subjective guesswork by using an empirical formula. It looks at four hard facts: Are employers actively posting jobs for this trade? Are wages rising above minimum wage? Does the current syllabus match modern job descriptions? Are students actually getting hired?

If job postings are high and wages are soaring, but the syllabus is far behind and placements are dropping, the course receives a high Gap Score. If this score stays high for 8 weeks in a row, the platform automatically triggers an administrative intervention.
GapScore(c, d) = 0.35 · Norm(V_12mo) + 0.20 · Norm(ΔW) + 0.25 · Dist_cosine(S_market, S_curriculum) − 0.20 · Norm(P_rate)
Diagram 3: Algorithmic Gap & Obsolescence Scoring Pipeline Source: docs/01-product/PRD.md & docs/02-architecture/BACKEND_ARCHITECTURE.md
Mathematical scoring pipeline: 4 empirical inputs are normalized, weighted, and evaluated over an 8-week persistence window to trigger automated curriculum revision dossiers.
⚙️ Mathematical Formulation & Parameter Weights
  • V_12mo (Weight 0.35 — 12-Month Vacancy Volume): Total deduplicated job vacancies in district d for course c over the past 12 months, normalized across statewide percentiles using min-max scaling: (V - V_min) / (V_max - V_min).
  • ΔW (Weight 0.20 — Regional Wage Premium): Average offered monthly wage relative to the Maharashtra statutory unskilled minimum wage (≈ ₹12,500/mo). High wage premiums indicate high-productivity, high-demand technical roles.
  • Dist_cosine (Weight 0.25 — Semantic Syllabus Drift): Cosine distance 1 - cos(θ) between the 384-dimensional vector embedding of emerging skill n-grams in job postings and the embedding of active course syllabus learning outcomes.
  • P_rate (Weight 0.20 — Historical Placement Rate): 4-quarter rolling placement rate verified from ITI CSV returns. This parameter is subtracted from the score to prevent false alarms on trades that still boast near-100% placement.
  • 8-Week Persistence Evaluator: To prevent momentary market noise from disrupting state education, a Gap Score > 0.60 must be maintained for ≥ 8 consecutive weekly runs before an automated Curriculum Modification Dossier is generated.

Chapter 5: Semantic Taxonomy Normalization & NLP Processing

Data Intelligence
💡 Plain English Concept — How do we make sense of messy, chaotic job postings?
Every company invents their own creative titles in job advertisements: one employer asks for a “Welding Rock Star,” another for a “TIG/MIG Fabricator,” and a third writes “Junior Metal Jointer.” If a computer system doesn't understand that these all refer to the same government qualification pack (Welder — NSQF Level 3), the demand data will be completely fragmented and useless!

MahaSkills acts like a multilingual universal translator. It takes chaotic job ads, strips out marketing buzzwords, extracts the underlying technical skill competencies, and matches them to official government qualification packs.
Diagram 4: 3-Stage Semantic Taxonomy Normalization Flow Source: docs/04-taxonomy/ & docs/06-data/DATA_MODEL.md
3-stage taxonomy normalization pipeline: Regex fast-path → spaCy entity extraction → dense vector similarity + Elasticsearch BM25 synonym expansion.
⚙️ 3-Stage Normalization Pipeline Mechanics
  • Stage 1 (Exact Regex Matching): Fast-path deterministic regex engine scanning for 2,200 canonical NSQF trade codes and standard DGT trade titles (e.g., ELE-01, FIT-02). Resolves ≈ 40% of clean vacancy listings in < 1ms.
  • Stage 2 (spaCy Statistical NER): Custom-trained statistical Named Entity Recognizer tokenizes job descriptions, extracts noun phrases, and identifies technical skills, machinery names, software packages, and certification requirements while filtering stop-words.
  • Stage 3 (Sentence-Transformer Embeddings & BM25 Synonyms): Unmapped skills are converted into 384-dimensional dense vectors using open-source all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and queried against the canonical skill taxonomy vector index, cross-referenced with Elasticsearch 8 BM25 synonym dictionaries containing 15,000+ technical descriptors.
  • Confidence Threshold & Quarantine Queue: Matches with cosine similarity ≥ 0.72 are automatically assigned to the canonical NSQF competence unit. Tokens scoring < 0.72 are safely routed to an administrative Unmapped Skills Quarantine Queue, where SSC analysts can map them via the UI with one click without needing model retraining.

Chapter 6: Data Privacy & Legal Compliance (DPDP Act 2023)

Cybersecurity & Law
💡 Plain English Concept — How do we protect student privacy and employer secrets?
India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 mandates strict criminal and financial penalties (up to ₹250 Crore) for leaking citizen personal information. Vocational students in rural ITIs must not have their roll numbers, names, or Aadhaar cards exposed online.

MahaSkills implements a zero-disk privacy enclave: the very instant an ITI principal uploads a placement report, student identification numbers are converted in computer memory into irreversible cryptographic codes. Even if a rogue hacker stole the entire database, they would see only random strings of letters and numbers with zero student identities.
Diagram 5: DPDP Act 2023 Cryptographic Pseudonymization Pipeline Source: docs/05-security/DATA_PRIVACY.md
Data privacy pipeline: Raw student roll numbers are converted to irreversible cryptographic hashes in volatile memory prior to database insertion. Raw PII touches zero disk blocks.
⚙️ Streaming HMAC-SHA256 Cryptographic Architecture
  • Volatile Memory Ingestion: Celery ingestion workers read uploaded placement CSV streams line-by-line entirely within volatile RAM. Unencrypted student identifiers (roll numbers, registration codes) never touch physical disk or database swap files.
  • Hardware Security Module (CloudHSM) Salt: Hashes are computed via HMAC-SHA256(student_id, DPDP_TENANT_SALT). The cryptographic salt is stored exclusively in an isolated CloudHSM enclave inaccessible to database administrators.
  • Non-Reversible Candidate Hashes: Database tables store only the resulting 64-character hex hash (candidate_hash), fully satisfying Section 8(6) of DPDP Act 2023.
  • Zero Employer Leakage: Enterprise hiring volumes, candidate requisitions, and salary offers are aggregated to district/sector medians before appearing on public analytics dashboards. Competitors cannot reverse-engineer proprietary corporate staffing strategies.
🏛️ DPDP Statutory Alignment Summary
By mathematically eliminating direct identifiers at the streaming network layer and isolating the cryptographic salt in CloudHSM, MahaSkills operates as a fully pseudonymized platform exempt from citizen de-anonymization vulnerabilities.

Chapter 7: Data Engineering, Ingestion & 10M-Record Table Partitioning

Data Engineering
💡 Plain English Concept — How does the system handle millions of records over years without slowing down?
Over 7 years across 36 districts and 417+ ITIs, the system accumulates more than 10 million individual student placement records and job listings. In an ordinary database, searching through 10 million rows takes minutes and eventually crashes the server.

MahaSkills organizes its database like a library with separate filing cabinets for each year: when an official asks for 2025 placement data, the database opens only the 2025 filing cabinet and completely ignores the other years. This keeps queries running in milliseconds forever.
Diagram 6: Automated Ingestion & Pipeline Orchestration (Apache Airflow) Source: docs/06-data/DATA_INGESTION.md
Data pipeline architecture: Scheduled Airflow DAGs isolate batch processing and scraping workloads, guaranteeing zero latency interference with the live user-facing FastAPI application.
⚙️ Airflow DAG Orchestration & Native Table Partitioning
  • Apache Airflow 2.8 Orchestration: Isolated scheduled DAGs manage batch ETL pipelines:
    • lmi_nightly_job_scraping: Executes daily at 02:00 IST (SLA ≤ 120m) across NCS and job portals with automated rate-limiting and proxy rotation.
    • placement_validation_worker: Streaming worker validating monthly ITI placement CSV batches line-by-line against schema boundaries.
    • taxonomy_nlp_enrichment: Executes daily at 04:00 IST (SLA ≤ 60m) to generate vector embeddings for newly ingested vacancies.
  • Atomic CSV Streaming & Zero Partial Commits: Placement uploads (up to 50,000 rows) are verified across candidate HMAC, course code validity, and wage ranges. If syntax or relational integrity errors occur, the entire batch status is marked REJECTED, corrupt rows are written to an error log, and zero corrupt data enters production tables.
  • PostgreSQL 16 Declarative Range Partitioning: The placement_records table is partitioned by RANGE (batch_year) into discrete annual tables (placement_records_2024, placement_records_2025). The query optimizer performs partition pruning, scanning only relevant cohorts.
  • Cold Data Parquet Archival: Job postings older than 24 months are automatically converted into compressed columnar Apache Parquet files and archived in cold S3 storage while active 12-month econometric data remains indexed.

Chapter 8: Deterministic RAG AI Assistant & Marathi-First Interface

Conversational AI
💡 Plain English Concept — Why a Marathi chatbot, and why can't it make things up?
Most vocational school principals in rural Maharashtra (in places like Gadchiroli, Nandurbar, or Beed) do not have time to learn complex data dashboards or write database queries. They need to ask simple questions in their mother tongue, Marathi: “Which courses in our district need updating for solar technology?”

Generic AI tools like ChatGPT often make up fake statistics (called “hallucination”). MahaSkills uses a technology called Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The AI is strictly forbidden from giving opinions; it is legally bound to act only as an audio-visual reader of our verified database, citing exact course codes and government batch numbers for every single sentence it speaks.
Diagram 7: Deterministic Grounded RAG Pipeline Architecture Source: docs/01-product/USER_STORIES.md & docs/02-architecture/SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md
Deterministic RAG pipeline: Eliminates hallucinations by extracting relational filters, executing hybrid SQL/vector lookups, and enforcing post-generation citation verification.
⚙️ Deterministic 4-Stage RAG Pipeline Architecture
  • 1. Intent & Entity Extraction: Analyzes user queries (in Marathi or English) to extract discrete SQL relational filters: district="Nagpur", sector="Green Energy", nsqf_level=4.
  • 2. Hybrid SQL & Vector Retrieval: Combines dense semantic vector retrieval via pgvector (384-dimensional embeddings of course outcomes) with sparse relational SQL queries fetching verified 12-month vacancy stats and placement numbers.
  • 3. Context-Constrained Injection: Retrieved facts are formatted into a strict system prompt for our local open-source LLM (Llama-3-8B executed via Ollama/vLLM): “You are an assistant for DSEEI Maharashtra. Answer exclusively from the provided database records. Every single statistic must cite the exact Course ID, District ID, and Batch Year.”
  • 4. Post-Generation Citation Regex Verification: A deterministic regex parser inspects the generated output before returning it to the user. If any claim lacks an exact verifiable citation ID from step 2, or if retrieval confidence is < 0.65, the system automatically falls back to: “Insufficient verified data available for this district-sector combination.”

Chapter 9: Multi-Tenant Security, RBAC/ABAC & STRIDE Cyber Defense

Platform Security
💡 Plain English Concept — Who is allowed to see and change what?
In a government system, a District Officer in Nagpur must not be able to secretly alter training budgets in Pune, and an ITI principal cannot artificially edit placement numbers to win awards. MahaSkills enforces strict digital identity badges and territorial locks so that every user sees only what they are legally authorized to manage.
Diagram 8: STRIDE Threat Assessment & Security Defense Perimeter Source: docs/05-security/THREAT_MODEL.md
STRIDE threat coverage: Explicit perimeter defenses address all 5 adversary attack vectors across authentication, data upload, scraping, and storage tiers.
⚙️ 7 Platform Roles & The STRIDE Threat Assessment Model
  • 7 Platform Roles: (1) State Policy Maker (DSEEI / MSInS), (2) District Skill Officer (DSEEGC), (3) ITI Principal, (4) ITI Vocational Instructor, (5) SSC Reviewer, (6) Industry Employer, (7) Candidate / Trainee.
  • Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC): Authenticated Keycloak JWTs contain custom realm claims (e.g., district_id: 2718). The FastAPI API Gateway and SQLAlchemy ORM automatically inject mandatory row-level security predicates (WHERE district_id = current_user.district_id) on every database transaction.
  • STRIDE Threat Coverage:
    • Spoofing (S): Forged identity tokens are blocked via RS256 signature verification against Keycloak JWKS public keys.
    • Tampering (T): Fraudulent ITI placement alterations are caught by SHA-256 batch checksums and statistical anomaly algorithms.
    • Repudiation (R): State approval decisions are permanently logged in an immutable, append-only audit_logs table with SHA-256 digital approval signatures.
    • Information Disclosure (I): Trainee PII is eliminated through zero-plaintext storage and HMAC-SHA256 pseudonymization.
    • Denial of Service (D): Upload zip-bombs are halted via 100MB streaming upload caps and Celery worker queue isolation.
    • Elevation of Privilege (E): Cross-district parameter tampering is blocked by TenantScopeGuard.

Chapter 10: Curriculum Governance State Machine & Faculty Protection

Administrative Workflow
💡 Plain English Concept — How does a computer recommendation become official government policy?
In the government, an algorithm cannot simply change classroom textbooks overnight without democratic accountability. Official curriculum changes require technical validation by national experts and administrative sanction by senior IAS officers.

Furthermore, when an obsolete trade is phased out, what happens to the government teachers who have taught it for 20 years? In government ITIs, teachers cannot be laid off! MahaSkills models the real administrative reality: it provides a 5-stage approval state machine, and automatically schedules affected faculty into government Advanced Training Institutes (ATIs) to learn new technologies before the updated courses launch.
Diagram 9: Curriculum Recommendation Governance State Machine Source: docs/02-architecture/BACKEND_ARCHITECTURE.md
Governance state machine: Guarantees no automated model can unilaterally alter official vocational curriculum without accredited Sector Skill Council validation and formal DSEEI administrative sanction.
⚙️ Statutory Approval Workflow & Operational Safeguards
  • 5-Stage Statutory State Machine:
    1. DRAFT: Generated automatically when a trade maintains a persistent Gap Score > 0.60 for ≥ 8 consecutive weeks.
    2. UNDER_SSC_REVIEW: Assigned to accredited Sector Skill Council technical evaluators for syllabus modification review.
    3. SSC_APPROVED: Formal technical sign-off by the SSC Technical Review Committee.
    4. DSEEI_APPROVAL: Administrative review and cryptographic digital sign-off by the DSEEI Joint Secretary.
    5. PUBLISHED: Released statewide to all 417+ ITIs, updating classroom timetables and equipment requisitions.
  • Two-Tiered Materiality Threshold:
    • Tier 1 (Minor Revisions — ≤20% Syllabus Delta): Adding an elective module or modern CAD software version. Signed off directly by the SSC Reviewer with administrative notification.
    • Tier 2 (Major Revisions / New Qualifications): Decommissioning an obsolete trade or introducing an entirely new NSQF qualification. Requires comprehensive evidence dossiers, interstate benchmarking, and DSEEI Joint Secretary sanction.
  • Mandated ≥60% Annual Faculty Retraining: When a trade is modified, the platform identifies affected faculty, maps their existing technical competencies to adjacent modern skills (e.g., ICE engine mechanics → EV battery assembly), and reserves seats in DSEEI Advanced Training Institutes (ATI) to hit the statutory ≥ 60% annual retraining coverage.
  • Automated ITI Equipment Gap Auditor: Cross-references NCVET Mandatory Workshop Equipment Standards against the ITI's uploaded asset registry. If critical equipment (e.g., High-Voltage Safety Mats, Digital Diagnostic Scanners) is absent, the system flags a Capex Modernization Line Item and inserts it into the District Annual Training Plan.

Chapter 13: Production Deployment, Air-Gapped Cloud & Disaster Recovery

DevOps & SRE
💡 Plain English Concept — Where does the software live, and what happens if a server dies?
MahaSkills is designed to run completely inside the Maharashtra State Data Centre (MahaGovCloud). It does not rely on third-party commercial APIs (like OpenAI or cloud databases in foreign countries) that can be blocked or charge unpredictable fees. Even if internet connectivity between the state data centre and the outside world is temporarily severed, all local analytics, databases, and AI models continue running smoothly.

  • Stateless Horizontal Auto-Scaling: FastAPI backend instances run inside stateless Docker containers orchestrated via Kubernetes / AWS ECS with Horizontal Pod Autoscaler (HPA) triggered at 70% CPU threshold.
